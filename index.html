The problem

The current route needs a safer option.

Students walking to CHE and New Educ may have to share a narrow route with vehicles, while the connecting road remains unfinished.

🚶

No designated sidewalk

People walking may need to use the edge of the road alongside moving vehicles.

▥

Unfinished connection

The connecting route from New Educ toward CHE is still unfinished in some sections.

♡

Safety concerns

Muddy, rocky, or uneven areas can make walking less comfortable, especially after rain.

👥

A shared concern

A safer walking route could benefit students, faculty, staff, and campus visitors.

Safer walks
build a stronger

The solution

A safer pedestrian path for CHE and New Educ.

CMU SafeWalk is an advocacy concept that supports a dedicated, accessible pedestrian path connecting the CHE and New Educ areas. Your support helps show that a safer walking route matters to the campus community.
